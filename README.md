## How projects are developed

Projects here remain traceable to work I actually completed in class.

**Coursework → concept → independent extension → usable project**

I distinguish between the original course requirements and later independent extensions. The goal is not to make assignments look larger than they were, but to show how classroom concepts became stronger engineering work over time.
